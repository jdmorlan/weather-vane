Privacy

Vane collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your devices

Your station's readings are downloaded from WeatherFlow and kept on your device, in the app's own storage, so Vane can compare this year with earlier ones. Your rain gauge log is kept the same way.

What iCloud carries

Your Tempest token, station and home point are synced between your own devices through iCloud key-value storage, so you set the app up once. Only your Apple account can read it.

What is sent, and to whom

To show your weather, Vane asks public weather services for data. Those requests contain your home point or the area around it, never anything that identifies you:

  • WeatherFlow (Tempest), with your read-only token, for your station's readings and forecast
  • The National Weather Service, the Storm Prediction Center, the Weather Prediction Center, NOAA nowCOAST and aviationweather.gov
  • NEXRAD radar files, from Unidata on Amazon Web Services
  • Open-Meteo, for model rain and the upper-air pattern
  • The US Drought Monitor, for your county's weekly rating
  • The Texas A&M Forest Service, for burn bans
  • YouTube and Space City Weather, only when you open the Videos or Blog pages

Each service has its own privacy policy for the requests it receives.
